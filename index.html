(01) Features

Everything a Short needs

One page, no installs. Cut, caption, title and export vertical video that is ready to post.

01

Auto captions

Speech to text runs inside your browser. English, Urdu script and Roman Urdu, with 20+ animated caption styles.

02

13 transitions

Dip to black, flash, zoom punch, slide, push, spin, glitch, wipe, iris, flip, blinds and more at every cut.

03

Camera recorder

Record yourself or your screen, then drop the take at the playhead, the start or the end of your timeline.

04

Text templates

Animated titles, lower thirds, countdowns, quote cards and stickers. Tap, type, done.

05

AI images

Generate pictures, backgrounds, QR codes and hook ideas, and drop them on top or in between clips.

06

Private and local

Your videos never leave this device. Projects are saved in your browser, ready when you come back.

Output

  

   
   
   
   
   
   
   
  

  
Format: MP4 (H.264/AAC) when your browser supports it, otherwise WebM. 

 

 
Clips / Reels

  
Add multiple videos at once, or create several clips from the current video. Every clip stays in this project. Preview, edit, and delete any clip without removing the others.

  

  
  

  

  

 

 
Quality

  

  

  
The turbo decoder reads MP4 / MOV (H.264 or HEVC) directly instead of playing the video in real time. If a file is not supported the exporter switches to the standard method by itself.

  

  

  

  
Edits (crop, background, captions) need re-encoding, so they cannot be bit-identical. For exact original quality use the two buttons under Convert.

 

 
Crop position

  

  

  

  
Drag the frame on the source view to reposition. Crop applies when background is Original.

 

 
Background

  

  
Blur, Solid and Gradient show the full frame, fitted to canvas width, over the background.

  

  

 

 
Captions

  
  
Captions are visible in the "Output preview" tab and in the export. Pick a template, then add timed captions.

  

   
   
   
   
  

  
Auto captions (speech to text)

  
Caption options (model, language, length)

  

  

  

  

  

  

  

  
  
  
Runs a Whisper model inside your browser (audio stays on your device). The model is downloaded once on first use, needs internet, and is slow on long clips. Language and translation apply to multilingual models only.

Fine-tune style

  

  

  

  

  

  

  

  

  

  

  

  

  

  

  
  

  

  

  

  

  

  
Word glow highlights each word in turn during the caption (karaoke style).

 

 
Text and elements (titles, images, shapes)

  

  
Drag text directly on the "Output preview" to move it. Pick a layer below to edit it. Show from/to = 0 means visible for the whole clip.

  

 
Text templates (animated titles)

  

  

  
Tap a template to add it at the playhead. Change the words in Layers, or drag it on the timeline to move it.

Elements

  

  
Tap an element to add it, then edit it in the Layers panel below (color, size, animation, timing). Drag it on the preview to move it.

 

 
Transitions

  

  

  
Applied automatically at every cut when clips are exported as one joined video. To pick a different transition for one cut, tap the small T button at the start of a clip on the timeline.

 

 
Generate image (AI)

  
  

  

  
  
Made by a free online AI service (pollinations.ai). Only your prompt is sent to it.

  

 

 
Generate background

  

  

  
  

  
Tap a background to choose: show it on top of the video, or add it to the timeline as an intro, outro or scene clip. Clip length is set above.
