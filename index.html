Clips / Reels

  
Add multiple videos at once, or create several clips from the current video. Every clip stays in this project. Preview, edit, and delete any clip without removing the others.

  

  
  

  

  

 

 
Quality

  

  

  
The turbo decoder reads MP4 / MOV (H.264 or HEVC) directly instead of playing the video in real time. If a file is not supported the exporter switches to the standard method by itself.

  

  

  

  
Edits (crop, background, captions) need re-encoding, so they cannot be bit-identical. For exact original quality use the two buttons under Convert.

 

 
Crop position

  

  

  

  
Drag the frame on the source view to reposition. Crop applies when background is Original.

 

 
Background

  

  
Blur, Solid and Gradient show the full frame, fitted to canvas width, over the background.

  

  

 

 
Captions

  
  
Captions are visible in the "Output preview" tab and in the export. Pick a template, then add timed captions.

Caption options (model, language, length)

  

  

  

  

  

  

  
  
  
Runs a Whisper model inside your browser (audio stays on your device). The model is downloaded once on first use, needs internet, and is slow on long clips. Language and translation apply to multilingual models only.

Elements

  

  
Tap an element to add it, then edit it in the Layers panel below (color, size, animation, timing). Drag it on the preview to move it.

 

 
Transitions

  

  

  
Applied automatically at every cut when clips are exported as one joined video. To pick a different transition for one cut, tap the small T button at the start of a clip on the timeline.

Privacy: your video is processed locally in your browser. It is not uploaded to a server by this app. Projects, edits, captions and clip selections are saved locally. Video files are stored in browser IndexedDB because localStorage cannot safely store large video files. Nothing is uploaded by this app.
